Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic account information such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service: authenticating your account, displaying your expense history, and generating spending summaries and category breakdowns. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which it will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services. Should this change in the future — for example, to support optional integrations — we will update this policy and notify users before any such sharing takes place.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
